What Your Website Needs Before You Pay for Traffic

Before spending on ads or promotions, make sure the landing experience is fast, clear, trustworthy and built to convert attention into a useful next step.

MaxImpress ArticlesUpdated September 2026No guaranteed outcomes
BEFORE ADS

Traffic magnifies what is already there

Paid traffic does not fix an unclear website. It sends more people into the same experience. If visitors cannot understand the offer, trust the business or complete the next step, more traffic can simply increase wasted spend.

Pre-traffic checklist

01

One clear promise

The landing page should explain the offer in language the target customer understands.

02

Mobile usability

Buttons, forms, navigation and text should work properly on small screens.

03

Fast loading

Heavy images and unstable layouts can lose visitors before the message appears.

04

Trust

Use real reviews, evidence, policies and transparent contact information where available.

05

Tracking plan

Decide what counts as success: form submission, message, purchase, booked call or another measurable action.

06

Follow-up

Make sure someone can actually respond to leads or enquiries after the traffic arrives.

BALANCE

Do not optimize for a perfect score only

Google’s current page-experience guidance recommends Core Web Vitals and an overall good experience, but also notes that no single score guarantees top search ranking. The same principle applies to conversion: technical metrics matter, but relevance and clarity still matter more than cosmetic perfection.
