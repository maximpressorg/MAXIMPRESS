WEBSITE • ESSENTIALS

Website Essentials: What Must Be Ready Before You Build

A website project becomes faster, cheaper and better when the business decisions are ready before the design begins. Prepare the ownership, content and conversion logic first.

MaxImpress ArticlesUpdated October 2026Practical guidance • No guaranteed outcomes
01 • OWNERSHIP

Control the assets

01

Domain

Register the domain under the person or organization that should legally control it. Keep renewal and recovery information current.

02

Hosting + access

Document where the site is hosted, who has administrator access, and how backups and renewals work.

03

Business email

Use a stable professional email for website accounts, Search Console, analytics and other critical services.

02 • CONTENT

Know what the site needs to say

01

Positioning

Write a one-sentence explanation of what you do, for whom and what the visitor should do next.

02

Proof

Collect real work examples, testimonials you are permitted to publish, credentials, policies, team information and contact details.

03

Calls to action

Decide the primary action for each key page: contact, book, buy, request a quote, subscribe or read more.

03 • TECHNICAL QUALITY

Build for real users and search

01

Mobile + accessibility

Layouts, buttons, forms and text must work cleanly on small screens and remain usable with keyboard and assistive technologies where possible.

02

Core Web Vitals

Google’s current Core Web Vitals focus on loading (LCP), responsiveness (INP) and visual stability (CLS). Performance should be measured, not guessed.

03

Search basics

Use crawlable pages, descriptive titles/headings, useful internal links, meaningful alt text and people-first content. Avoid spammy SEO tactics.

04 • MEASUREMENT

Know what success means

01

Analytics

Track the actions that matter—qualified inquiries, purchases, downloads, bookings, contact clicks—not just visits.

02

Search Console

Use Google Search Console to inspect indexing and identify technical/search visibility issues.

03

Maintenance owner

Assign responsibility for updates, security, expired content and broken links after launch.
