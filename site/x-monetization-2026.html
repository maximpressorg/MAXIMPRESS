X Monetization in 2026

A practical, current guide to X creator monetization: what is changing, how eligibility works, what “qualified impressions” mean, and what creators should prepare before applying.

MaxImpress ArticlesUpdated September 2026No guaranteed outcomes
START HERE

The short version

X monetization is not a single switch. A creator needs the right paid subscription, an eligible account, original content, recent qualifying reach, good standing, and an approved payout setup. Meeting visible thresholds can make an account eligible to apply, but X still reviews the account and controls admission and payouts.

As of September 1, 2026, X is transitioning away from Creator Revenue Sharing. New enrollment into the old program closed on August 7; existing members can continue earning through September 7; and X says rollout for existing Revenue Sharing members to apply to Original Content Rewards begins September 8. This timing matters because old 5-million-impression guidance belongs to the retiring program, while the new program uses a different eligibility model.

What the new Original Content Rewards model focuses on

01

Original work

X describes eligible work as content you personally create or substantially transform with your own voice, perspective, expertise, reporting, analysis, design, image, video or commentary.

02

Qualified reach

The new program measures qualified impressions from Premium users on the Home Timeline. The official definition includes unique Premium-user impressions where at least half of the post is visible.

03

Recent performance

Eligibility uses a rolling 90-day window. Old performance eventually drops out, so a single strong month does not permanently solve the requirement.

04

Account quality

The account must remain in good standing and continue complying with X rules, monetization standards and program integrity requirements.

05

Payout readiness

Approved creators must use the payout method available for their location and complete identity verification. X currently directs U.S. users toward X Money and non-U.S. users toward Stripe.

06

Ongoing eligibility

Monetization is not “set and forget.” X says participants can be reviewed again and must keep meeting content and conduct requirements.

ELIGIBILITY

Current eligibility checklist

Original Content Rewards - current X Help Center criteria

At the time of this update, X lists: age 18+, a supported location, a Personal or Business account in good standing, an active Premium / Premium+ / Premium Business subscription, at least 500 verified followers, at least 500,000 Home Timeline impressions from verified users in the previous 90 days, and active posting of original content. X says impressions on replies are excluded from the 500,000 eligibility requirement.

Older monetization guidance may still mention a 5-million-impression figure. That number belongs to the retiring Creator Revenue Sharing framework; the current Original Content Rewards eligibility page uses a different threshold and a narrower definition of qualifying reach.

Eligibility is not approval. X explicitly says meeting the listed requirements does not guarantee admission.

IMPRESSIONS

Qualified impressions are not the same as raw views

A raw analytics total can include many kinds of exposure. Original Content Rewards uses a narrower definition. X excludes repeated impressions from the same account on the same post, paid or promoted impressions, artificially generated impressions and fraudulent impressions. That is why “I have enough views” and “I have enough qualified impressions” are not always the same statement.

Replies can still be valuable for discovery, relationships and follower growth, but X’s current eligibility page says impressions on replies are excluded from the 500,000 Home Timeline requirement. A responsible strategy therefore uses replies to build visibility while relying on original Home Timeline posts for qualifying reach.

What can make content ineligible

A

Copied content

Re-uploading or reproducing another creator’s work without a meaningful original contribution.

B

Minimal changes

Small edits, filters, speed changes or text overlays that do not materially transform someone else’s work.

C

Low-value aggregation

Compilations that mainly collect other people’s material without substantial new analysis or framing.

D

Artificial activity

Bots, automated engagement, fraudulent views or tools intended to manipulate recommendation or payout systems.

E

Policy problems

Content or conduct that violates X rules, monetization standards, copyright requirements or other program terms.

F

Monetization-only content

X says content exclusively focused on monetization coaching, monetization discussion or maximizing payouts is not eligible for Original Content Rewards.
