WEBSITE • DECISION GUIDE

Which Website Is Right for Me? Start With the Job the Website Must Do

The right website type is determined by your objective—not by whichever design is trending. Start by defining what the visitor must understand and what action you want them to take.

MaxImpress ArticlesUpdated October 2026Practical guidance • No guaranteed outcomes
01 • PERSONAL / CREATOR

When your name is the brand

01

Best fit

A portfolio-led site with a strong introduction, work/examples, proof, media or client results, services/collaborations and a direct contact path.

02

Do not overbuild

A creator rarely needs a complicated corporate sitemap. Clarity and proof matter more than dozens of pages.

02 • SERVICE BUSINESS

When you need inquiries and trust

01

Best fit

A conversion-led business site: Home, Services, About, proof/case studies, FAQs or process, contact/brief, policies and location information where relevant.

02

Add landing pages carefully

Use focused landing pages for campaigns or specific services instead of sending every visitor to a generic homepage.

03 • ORGANIZATION

When governance and public information matter

01

Best fit

A structured organizational site with mission, leadership, programs/services, reports or resources, news/updates, contact information, policies and accessibility-minded navigation.

02

Keep evidence current

Old reports, broken contact details and abandoned news pages damage trust. Ownership of updates should be assigned internally.

04 • CONTENT / MEDIA

When publishing itself is the product

01

Best fit

A content-led architecture with categories, search, author/profile pages, strong internal linking, clear dates and mobile performance.

02

Plan discovery

Use descriptive titles, useful page structure and people-first content so readers and search engines can understand each page.
