Customized Research for Your Business

We provide customized social media research based on your specific business needs. Our work uses publicly available information across multiple social platforms and organizes it into a clear, practical format.

01

What We Do

  • Search publicly available information across multiple social media platforms
  • Identify relevant people, businesses, posts, discussions, and opportunities
  • Filter findings by location, industry, audience, keywords, or other criteria
  • Organize results into a clear and usable format
  • Support lead generation, market research, outreach, and business development
02

Construction Company Example

A construction company may need information about people who are:

  • Planning to build a house
  • Looking for construction services
  • Searching for contractors
  • Discussing renovation or property development
  • Requesting construction-related recommendations
We research relevant public posts and discussions, filter the findings according to your criteria, and provide a structured list of potential opportunities.

Service Scope & Compliance

  • Whether a lead responds, negotiates, or completes a deal is entirely between you and that lead.
  • MaxImpress does not guarantee conversions, agreements, or completed transactions.
  • Our research is conducted legally and uses only publicly available, official, or openly accessible information.
