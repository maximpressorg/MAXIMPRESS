RESULTS & PROOF

Results and Payout Examples

Understand how to evaluate payout screenshots, growth results, and case studies without turning someone else’s outcome into an earnings promise.

Use results as evidence, not as a promise

Creator payouts and account growth vary substantially. A useful result shows what happened on a specific account; it does not guarantee the same outcome for another account.

01

First payout

A first payout can show that a creator reached a monetization milestone, but the amount depends on that account’s qualifying performance.

02

Ongoing payouts

Recurring payouts show continuity over time, but they can rise or fall as content performance, audience response, program rules, and account eligibility change.

03

Growth trajectory

Before-and-after analytics can demonstrate movement in reach, followers, or engagement when the comparison uses the same metric and time window.

04

Client evidence

Only results that genuinely belong to MaxImpress clients should be presented as MaxImpress results. Public third-party screenshots must not be relabeled as client proof.
