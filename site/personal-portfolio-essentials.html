What Should a Personal Portfolio Include?

A personal portfolio should prove who you are, what you do, what you have done, and how someone can contact or work with you.

MaxImpress ArticlesUpdated September 2026No guaranteed outcomes
PORTFOLIO CORE

The essential sections

01

Clear introduction

Name, role, specialty and a short explanation of the value you bring.

02

About

A concise professional story that adds context without becoming a full autobiography.

03

Selected work

Projects, case studies, publications, campaigns, products or other evidence relevant to your goals.

04

Results and proof

Testimonials, measurable outcomes, media, credentials or references when they are genuine and appropriate.

05

Services or collaboration

If you are available for work, explain what kind of projects or opportunities make sense.

06

Contact

Give visitors a professional, low-friction way to reach you.

TRUST

Show evidence, not claims

“I am excellent at growth” is a claim. A clear case study showing the problem, work and outcome is evidence. Portfolios become more credible when visitors can see what you actually did and when exaggerated promises are removed.

MAINTENANCE

Keep it current

A portfolio is a living asset. Replace outdated examples, update your role and contact paths, remove broken links, and make sure the work shown reflects the direction you want next.
